# Visit Singapore: Essential Travel Information

## Climate
Singapore has a tropical rainforest climate with uniformly high temperatures, high humidity, and abundant rainfall year-round. Temperatures usually range from 26°C to 31°C (79°F to 88°F). Pack light and breathable clothing.

## Language
Singapore has four official languages: English, Malay, Mandarin, and Tamil. English is widely spoken and is the primary language of business and education.

## Currency
The currency used is the Singapore Dollar (SGD). Credit cards are widely accepted, but it's good to carry some cash for hawker centres and small shops.

## Connectivity
Wi-Fi is widely available in hotels, cafes, and public areas. You can purchase a prepaid SIM card at Changi Airport or convenience stores, or set up an eSIM before arrival.

## Visa
Most nationalities do not require a visa for a short stay (usually 30 to 90 days), but all visitors must fill out the SG Arrival Card online within 3 days before arriving.

## Emergency Contacts
- Police: 999
- Ambulance and Fire: 995
