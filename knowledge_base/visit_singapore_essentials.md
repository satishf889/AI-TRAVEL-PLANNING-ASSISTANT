# Visit Singapore: Essential Travel Information

## 1. Climate and Weather
Singapore is situated just 1 degree north of the equator and experiences a tropical rainforest climate year-round with no distinct dry season.
- **Temperature**: Average daily temperatures range between 25°C (77°F) and 32°C (90°F).
- **Humidity**: Consistently high, typically between 70% and 90%.
- **Monsoons**: 
  - *Northeast Monsoon (November to January)*: Characterised by frequent, heavy afternoon showers and overcast skies.
  - *Inter-monsoon periods (April-May, October-November)*: Afternoon thunderstorms are common.
- **Clothing Advice**: Light, breathable cotton or linen clothing is recommended. Always carry an umbrella or light raincoat, and bring a light cardigan/jacket for air-conditioned malls and MRT trains.

## 2. Currency, Payments & Tipping
- **Currency**: Singapore Dollar (SGD / S$). Notes come in $2, $5, $10, $50, $100, $1000.
- **Card Acceptance**: Singapore is an overwhelmingly cashless society. Contactless Visa, Mastercard, Apple Pay, and Google Pay are accepted on public transport (SimplyGo), taxis, restaurants, and retail shops.
- **Cash Needs**: Cash (SGD) is still useful for small hawker stalls and traditional neighbourhood markets.
- **Tipping**: Tipping is generally not customary in Singapore. Most sit-down restaurants and hotels automatically add a 10% service charge and prevailing 9% Goods and Services Tax (GST).

## 3. Public Transportation
Singapore's public transport system is fast, clean, and affordable.
- **Mass Rapid Transit (MRT)**: The MRT rail network connects all major districts, attractions, and Changi Airport. Trains run daily from approx. 5:30 AM to midnight.
- **SimplyGo**: Tap any foreign contactless Mastercard/Visa credit or debit card, or phone wallet directly at MRT gantries and on buses. No card purchase required.
- **Singapore Tourist Pass (STP)**: Unlimited travel passes for tourists:
  - 1-Day Pass: SGD 10 (+ SGD 10 refundable deposit)
  - 2-Day Pass: SGD 16
  - 3-Day Pass: SGD 20
- **Taxis and Ride-Hailing**: Taxis are metered and safe. Prominent ride-hailing apps include Grab, Gojek, Tada, and CDG Zig.

## 4. Connectivity & Power
- **SIM Cards & eSIMs**: Prepaid tourist SIMs (Singtel, StarHub, M1) are available at Changi Airport and 7-Eleven convenience stores (passport registration required).
- **Free Public Wi-Fi**: "Wireless@SGx" offers free Wi-Fi in thousands of public hotspots across Singapore.
- **Electrical Plugs**: Type G three-pin rectangular plugs (230V, 50Hz), identical to the UK standard.

## 5. Entry Requirements & Visas
- **SG Arrival Card (SGAC)**: All travelers must submit the free electronic SG Arrival Card with health declaration online within 3 days prior to arrival via the official ICA portal (eservices.ica.gov.sg).
- **Passport Validity**: Minimum 6 months validity required from date of entry.

## 6. Important Laws & Local Regulations
Singapore is famous for strict cleanliness and safety laws:
- **Chewing Gum**: The sale and import of chewing gum is prohibited.
- **Littering & Jaywalking**: Heavy fines starting from SGD 300 for littering or smoking outside designated zones.
- **Vaping**: Electronic vaporizers (e-cigarettes) are strictly banned; possession and importation carry heavy fines.
- **Drugs**: Singapore imposes severe zero-tolerance penalties, including capital punishment, for drug trafficking.

## 7. Emergency Contacts
- **Police**: 999
- **Ambulance / Fire (SCDF)**: 995
- **Non-Emergency Ambulance**: 1777
- **Tourist Hotline (STB)**: 1800 736 2000 (toll-free in Singapore)
