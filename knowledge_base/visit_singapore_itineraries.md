# Visit Singapore: Sample Itineraries

## 1-Day Itinerary: The Highlights
- **Morning:** Visit Gardens by the Bay (Flower Dome and Cloud Forest).
- **Afternoon:** Explore Marina Bay Sands and walk across the Helix Bridge.
- **Evening:** Enjoy a local dinner at Makansutra Gluttons Bay and watch the Spectra light and water show.

## 3-Day Itinerary: Culture & City
- **Day 1:** Marina Bay area (Gardens by the Bay, ArtScience Museum) and dinner at Lau Pa Sat.
- **Day 2:** Cultural exploration in Chinatown (Buddha Tooth Relic Temple), Little India, and Kampong Glam.
- **Day 3:** Shopping on Orchard Road and a relaxing evening at the Singapore Botanic Gardens.

## 5-Day Itinerary: Family Fun & Nature
- **Day 1 & 2:** Sentosa Island (Universal Studios Singapore, S.E.A. Aquarium, beaches).
- **Day 3:** Singapore Zoo and Night Safari.
- **Day 4:** Jewel Changi Airport and East Coast Park.
- **Day 5:** Museums and Civic District (National Gallery Singapore, Merlion Park).
