# Visit Singapore: Sample Itineraries

## 1. The Classic 3-Day Singapore Itinerary (Weather-Adaptive)

### Day 1: Modern Icons & Marina Bay
- **Morning (Outdoor)**: Start at Merlion Park for iconic waterfront photos of Marina Bay Sands. Walk across Jubilee Bridge to the Civic District and National Gallery Singapore.
- **Afternoon (Indoor / Climate-Controlled)**: Head to Gardens by the Bay. Explore the air-conditioned Flower Dome and Cloud Forest (featuring the 35-meter indoor waterfall).
  - *Rain Contingency*: If it rains, remain inside the Domes or explore The Shoppes at Marina Bay Sands and ArtScience Museum.
- **Evening (Outdoor / Covered)**: Watch the free Garden Rhapsody light and sound show at the Supertree Grove (7:45 PM & 8:45 PM). Dine at Satay by the Bay or Lau Pa Sat Satay Street.

### Day 2: Cultural Enclaves & Heritage Food Trail
- **Morning (Chinatown)**: Visit the Buddha Tooth Relic Temple and Sri Mariamman Temple. Grab brunch at Maxwell Food Centre (try Tian Tian Chicken Rice).
- **Afternoon (Kampong Gelam & Bugis)**: Stroll down Haji Lane for boutique shopping and vibrant murals. Visit the Sultan Mosque on Arab Street.
  - *Rain Contingency*: If afternoon thunderstorms occur, browse the air-conditioned National Museum of Singapore or Bugis Junction.
- **Evening (Little India & Night Safari)**: Explore Little India's spice markets and dinner at Mustafa/Tekka Centre. Take a shuttle to the world-famous Night Safari (guided tram ride through nocturnal wildlife habitats).

### Day 3: Island Adventure & Skyline Views
- **Morning to Afternoon (Sentosa Island)**: Take the Singapore Cable Car or Sentosa Express from VivoCity. 
  - *Sunny Weather*: Relax at Siloso Beach, visit Universal Studios Singapore or Adventure Cove Waterpark.
  - *Rainy Weather*: Visit S.E.A. Aquarium (one of the world's largest aquariums) or Madame Tussauds Singapore.
- **Evening (Jewel Changi Airport)**: Before flying out, visit Jewel Changi Airport to witness the 40-meter indoor HSBC Rain Vortex and stroll through the Shiseido Forest Valley.

## 2. Express 1-Day Highlights Itinerary
- **09:00 - 11:30**: Singapore Botanic Gardens (UNESCO World Heritage Site) & National Orchid Garden.
- **12:00 - 14:00**: Lunch in Chinatown (Maxwell Food Centre) & Buddha Tooth Relic Temple.
- **14:30 - 17:30**: Gardens by the Bay (Flower Dome & Cloud Forest biomes).
- **18:00 - 21:00**: Marina Bay Sands SkyPark Observation Deck followed by dinner at Lau Pa Sat.

## 3. 5-Day Comprehensive & Family Itinerary
- **Day 1**: Arrival & Marina Bay Highlights (Gardens by the Bay, Sands SkyPark).
- **Day 2**: Heritage Deep Dive (Chinatown, Little India, Kampong Gelam).
- **Day 3**: Wildlife Parks (Singapore Zoo, River Wonders, Mandai Bird Paradise).
- **Day 4**: Sentosa Island (Universal Studios Singapore, S.E.A. Aquarium, Wings of Time light show).
- **Day 5**: Green Spaces & Shopping (Singapore Botanic Gardens, Orchard Road, Jewel Changi Airport).
