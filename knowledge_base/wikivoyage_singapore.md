# Wikivoyage Singapore Travel Guide

## Districts and Neighbourhoods
- **Civic District & Marina Bay:** The modern core with historic colonial buildings and stunning skyscrapers.
- **Chinatown:** Rich in heritage, temples, and bustling markets.
- **Little India & Kampong Glam:** Vibrant enclaves representing Indian and Malay cultures.
- **Orchard Road:** The primary shopping belt of the city.
- **Sentosa:** A resort island dedicated to entertainment and beaches.

## Major Attractions
- Merlion Park
- Gardens by the Bay
- Universal Studios Singapore
- Singapore Flyer

## Transportation Options
- **MRT (Mass Rapid Transit):** The most efficient and cost-effective way to get around.
- **Buses:** Extensive network covering areas not reached by MRT.
- **Taxis & Ride-hailing:** Grab and Gojek are widely used. Taxis are metered and reliable.

## Food and Dining
Singapore is famous for its hawker centres, offering delicious and affordable local food. Must-try dishes include:
- Hainanese Chicken Rice
- Chilli Crab
- Laksa
- Char Kway Teow

## Practical Travel Guidance
Singapore is incredibly safe, clean, and well-organized. Be mindful of strict laws, such as the prohibition of chewing gum and heavy fines for littering.
