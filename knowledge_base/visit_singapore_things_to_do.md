# Visit Singapore: Things to Do

## 1. Top Iconic Attractions
- **Gardens by the Bay**:
  - *Flower Dome*: The world's largest glass greenhouse featuring flora from Mediterranean and semi-arid regions.
  - *Cloud Forest*: Features a 35-meter indoor waterfall, lush mountain vegetation, and aerial walkways.
  - *Supertree Grove*: 18 vertical gardens (25 to 50 meters tall). OCBC Skyway connects two Supertrees for elevated panoramic views.
  - *Hours*: Outdoor gardens 5:00 AM – 2:00 AM (Free); Conservatories 9:00 AM – 9:00 PM (Ticketed).
- **Marina Bay Sands**:
  - *SkyPark Observation Deck*: 57 floors above ground, offering 360-degree views of Singapore skyline and Singapore Strait.
  - *ArtScience Museum*: Lotus-inspired architectural masterpiece hosting immersive exhibitions like teamLab's "Future World".
- **Jewel Changi Airport**:
  - *HSBC Rain Vortex*: World's tallest indoor waterfall (40 meters).
  - *Canopy Park*: Top floor featuring Hedge Maze, Mirror Maze, and Walking Nets. Open 24/7 (Canopy Park attractions 10:00 AM – 10:00 PM).
- **Singapore Flyer**:
  - Giant observation wheel standing 165 meters tall, offering 30-minute rotation flights.

## 2. Nature & Wildlife Parks (Mandai Wildlife Reserve)
- **Singapore Zoo**: World-renowned "open-concept" rainforest zoo home to over 4,200 animals.
- **Night Safari**: The world's first nocturnal wildlife park. Features guided tram rides and walking trails (open from 7:15 PM to midnight).
- **River Wonders**: Asia's first river-themed wildlife park featuring giant pandas (Kai Kai and Jia Jia) and the Amazon Flooded Forest.
- **Bird Paradise**: Brand-new state-of-the-art aviary with 8 walk-through aviaries showcasing 3,500 birds.
- **Singapore Botanic Gardens**: Singapore's first UNESCO World Heritage Site (established in 1859). Features the National Orchid Garden with over 1,000 species and 2,000 hybrids. Open 5:00 AM – midnight daily (free admission to main gardens).

## 3. Arts, Culture & Museums
- **National Gallery Singapore**: Housed in the restored City Hall and Former Supreme Court, holding the largest public collection of modern Southeast Asian art.
- **Asian Civilisations Museum (ACM)**: Explores the ancestral cultures and maritime trade history of Southeast, East, and South Asia.
- **Peranakan Museum**: Showcases the rich material culture, porcelain, and beadwork of the Peranakan community.
- **Buddha Tooth Relic Temple (Chinatown)**: Tang-dynasty styled Buddhist temple holding a sacred tooth relic of Buddha. Free admission, respectful dress required.

## 4. Sentosa Island & Family Entertainment
- **Universal Studios Singapore (USS)**: 6 themed zones including Sci-Fi City (Battlestar Galactica rollercoasters), Ancient Egypt, and The Lost World.
- **S.E.A. Aquarium**: Home to over 100,000 marine animals across 1,000 species, featuring giant manta rays and hammerhead sharks.
- **Adventure Cove Waterpark**: High-speed water slides, lazy river, and snorkeling with 20,000 tropical fish at Rainbow Reef.
