# Visit Singapore: Things to Do

## Attractions
- **Marina Bay Sands:** Iconic integrated resort with an observation deck and infinity pool.
- **Gardens by the Bay:** Futuristic park featuring Supertree Grove and climate-controlled conservatories.
- **Jewel Changi Airport:** A nature-themed retail and entertainment complex featuring the Rain Vortex indoor waterfall.

## Arts & Culture
- **National Gallery Singapore:** Houses the largest public collection of modern art in Southeast Asia.
- **Kampong Glam:** Historic district known for the Sultan Mosque and trendy Haji Lane.
- **Chinatown & Little India:** Heritage enclaves offering a mix of temples, street art, and traditional eateries.

## Nature & Wildlife
- **Singapore Botanic Gardens:** A UNESCO World Heritage site perfect for a morning stroll.
- **Singapore Zoo & Night Safari:** Renowned for their open-concept enclosures and nocturnal wildlife tours.
- **MacRitchie Reservoir:** A popular spot for hiking and the scenic TreeTop Walk.
