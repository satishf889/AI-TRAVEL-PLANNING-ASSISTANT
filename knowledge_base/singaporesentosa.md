# Singapore/Sentosa

## Overview
Sentosa is an island just off the southern coast of Singapore. The district also includes Harbourfront and the Southern Islands. Formerly a malarial swamp known as Pulau Belakang Mati ("The Island of Death From Behind" in Malay), it was renamed Sentosa (Malay for "tranquillity") and redeveloped into a major tourist destination popular as a quick island getaway.

Resorts World Sentosa is the main complex on the island and comprises Universal Studios, the Singapore Oceanarium, Adventure Cove Waterpark, a casino, shopping malls and hotels. At the eastern tip of the island is Sentosa Cove, an upscale residential neighbourhood where modest bungalows start at about $10 million.

Small southern islets near Sentosa include Kusu Island, St. John's Island, the Sisters Islands and Pulau Hantu. The first three can be reached by public ferry; Pulau Hantu requires a chartered boat.

The island is open to the public 24 hours a day, 365 days a year. Entry fees for admission into Sentosa via most modes of transport were reinstated on 1 April 2023; however, walking or biking in is free, hotel guests can have admission waived, and Sentosa Islander "Insider" members get free entry and parking.

## Getting there

- General
  - Island admission fees were reinstated on 1 April 2023; walking or cycling in remains free. Hotel guests may have admission waived and Sentosa Islander "Insider" members get free entry and parking.
  - The island is open 24/7, year-round.

- By train (Sentosa Express)
  - Take the North East (Purple) Line or Circle (Orange) Line of the MRT to Harbourfront MRT station. Go to Level 3 via Lobby L to take the Sentosa Express monorail.
  - Fare including Sentosa island entry is $4, payable at the gates by tapping an EZ-Link or credit card. Children under 0.9 m tall are free.
  - The monorail operates from 7 AM to midnight.
  - Tickets are only checked at Harbourfront MRT station; taking the monorail from Sentosa back to the mainland is free.

- By bus
  - Bus 123 connects Orchard Road and Tiong Bahru directly to Sentosa.
  - Bus RWS8 connects from Harbourfront/VivoCity.
  - Standard bus fares apply.

- By cable car
  - From Harbourfront Tower II via the Mount Faber line. The Mount Faber Line covers Mount Faber Peak, Harbourfront and Sentosa.
  - The Sentosa Line (Imbiah Lookout Cable Car Station) covers Merlion (closed), Imbiah Lookout and Siloso Point Cable Car Stations. Imbiah Lookout is within a 3–5 minute walk from Sentosa Cable Car Station.

- By car or taxi
  - Entry fee for cars and taxis ranges from $2 (noon–2 PM, 5 PM–7 AM) to $6 (7 AM–noon, 2–5 PM).
  - Island admission can be auto-deducted via the CashCard in the in-vehicle unit (I.U.) or paid in cash at the gantry counters.
  - Parking lots: Beach Station, Imbiah, Tanjong Beach, Palawan Beach and Sentosa Cove Village Parks.
  - Carpark charges: $1.20 per hour (7 AM–5 PM) and $2.40 per entry (5 PM–7 AM).

- By ferry
  - For the Southern Islands, go to Marina South Pier for ferries to the Sisters' Islands, Kusu Island and St John's Island.
  - On weekends and public holidays there are direct ferries from Sentosa Jetty @ Cove to Lazarus Seringat Jetty (roundtrip $20/$15 adults/children; family package $64). Departure from Sentosa Jetty @ Cove: 9 AM, 11 AM, 1 PM and 3 PM. Return departures from Lazarus Seringat Jetty: 10:30 AM, 12:30 PM, 2:30 PM and 5:30 PM.
  - To reach Pulau Hantu you must charter a boat from West Coast Pier or Marina South Pier.

- On foot or bicycle
  - Admission by foot via the Sentosa Boardwalk or by cycling is free. The boardwalk starts from VivoCity, is mostly shaded and includes travellators.

## Getting around Sentosa
- Most transport around Sentosa is free (exceptions: taxis, private-hire vehicles and the cable car). This includes taking the Sentosa Express back to the mainland.
- Beach Station is the terminus of the Sentosa Express and the island's primary transport hub, within walking distance of Siloso and Palawan beaches.
- Beach trams (open-air) shuttle between Beach Station and all three beaches every 10 minutes.
- Three island shuttle buses (A, B and C) operate for longer distances; all run every 15 minutes. Buses A and B: 7 AM–12:10 AM daily. Bus C: 8 AM–10 PM daily.
- An internal shuttle service operates within Sentosa Cove for residents with access cards only.

## See and do

- General notes
  - Sentosa promotes a Fun Pass token system that gives small discounts (5–8%) on some attractions if purchased in large packages. Most big-ticket attractions do not accept the Fun Pass; leftover tokens cannot be exchanged back and are useless outside Sentosa. Most attractions accept credit cards.

- Resorts World Sentosa
  - Reachable via Resorts World station (formerly Waterfront station) on the monorail or via bus RWS8 from Harbourfront and other points.
  - The complex includes Universal Studios Singapore, the Singapore Oceanarium (formerly S.E.A. Aquarium), the Maritime Experiential Museum and Adventure Cove Waterpark, plus a casino, shopping and hotels.
  - Multi-Park Passes are available. The most comprehensive two-day pass (including Universal Studios Singapore, S.E.A. Aquarium, Maritime Experiential Museum and Adventure Cove Waterpark) is listed at $188/$136 for a two-day visit; it is noted as more expensive than purchasing individual tickets combined but allows switching between attractions.

- Universal Studios Singapore
  - Hours listed as 11 AM–6 PM daily.
  - The first Universal Studios theme park in Southeast Asia. It is smaller than other Universal parks and can get crowded; expect long lines or consider the Express Pass.
  - Seven themed zones: Hollywood, New York, Sci-Fi City, Ancient Egypt, The Lost World, Far Far Away and Madagascar.
  - Highlight: Battlestar Galactica, described as the world's tallest "dueling" rollercoaster with two tracks—"Cylon" (suspended, loops and inversions) and "Human" (seated, up to 90 km/h).
  - Inclement weather can affect operations, though some rides may remain open.
  - One-day pass prices (updated Aug 2023): $82 for adults (13 and above) and $61 for children (4–12).
  - Tickets can be purchased from trusted local travel agents for a discount.

- Singapore Oceanarium (formerly S.E.A. Aquarium)
  - Hours listed as 10 AM–5 PM daily.
  - Described as one of the world’s largest aquariums and home to species such as manta rays, enormous goliath grouper, Napoleon wrasse, and other marine animals.

## Practical tips and notes
- Expect crowds, especially on weekends and during school holidays; queues can be long in hot mid-day conditions.
- Many attractions are family-oriented; definitions used: "child" generally refers to ages 3–12 (specific ticketing ages vary by attraction).
- Check attraction opening hours and ticket prices before visiting, as details (hours, prices, availability) are subject to change.
