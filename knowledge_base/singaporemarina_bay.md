# Singapore/Marina Bay

## Overview
Marina Bay is Singapore's newest district, created on reclaimed land just east of Riverside. It includes the areas east of Shenton Way and Esplanade Drive. The bay is the body of water created by reclaiming land around the mouth of the Singapore River and blocked off from the sea by the Marina Barrage. Marina Bay mixes business, shopping, entertainment and culture in one accessible district. Marina Bay Sands and the super trees of Gardens by the Bay are major landmarks.

## Getting there
- The northern side of the bay is best accessed via the Circle MRT Line, with stations at:
  - Esplanade (for Suntec City)
  - Promenade (for Suntec City and Millenia Walk; also on the Downtown Line)
  - Bayfront
  - Marina Bay (also on the North–South and Thomson–East Coast lines)
  - Prince Edward Island
- You can walk for approximately 15 minutes through the underground (air-conditioned) CityLink shopping mall from Raffles City (at the City Hall MRT station where the North–South and East–West lines intersect).
- A less-touristed route to Merlion Park is through an air-conditioned linkway within the Fullerton Hotel complex (near Raffles Place) towards the Bay.
- The southern half (Marina Bay Sands and Gardens by the Bay) is best accessed via the Downtown MRT Line, with stations at:
  - Bayfront (for Marina Bay Sands and Gardens by the Bay)
  - Downtown (for Lau Pa Sat and the Financial District)
- The Gardens by the Bay station of the Thomson–East Coast line is located on the southern side of the Gardens; walking from here to Bayfront is noted as a good option for exploring the Gardens.

### By boat
- Marina Bay Cruise Centre (about 800 m from MRT Marina South Pier). Most cruise ships visiting Singapore dock here, though some use the terminal at Harbourfront instead. The MRT station is connected but is a lengthy walk; taxis are recommended for passengers with suitcases. (updated Jun 2022)
- Marina South Pier (MRT Marina South Pier). Local ferries to the Southern Islands of Kusu and St. John depart here. The pier also hosts the small but free Maritime Heritage Gallery museum, has ticket offices for local ferry companies and shops for snacks and drinks. Not to be confused with the larger Cruise Centre nearby. (updated Jun 2022)

## Getting around
- Suntec City, Marina Square, Millenia Walk and the Esplanade in the north are linked by underground malls or covered walkways.
- The Marina Bay Sands can be reached from the northern half by walking south for about 20 minutes on the Helix Bridge, which has several viewing platforms overlooking the bay.

## Attractions

### Marina Bay Sands and nearby
- Marina Bay Sands Casino
  - Address: 4 Bayfront Ave
  - Phone: +65 6688 8868
  - Hours: 24 hours daily
  - Notes: Singapore's second casino; visitors can enter for free, but locals and residents pay $150 to enter. Age limit 21 and ID required. (updated Jun 2019)

- Sands SkyPark (Marina Bay Sands)
  - Location note: To get to the ticket counter, get down the escalator outside the lobby of tower 3.
  - Hours: 10AM–10PM daily
  - Notes: SkyPark is perched 55 stories above the ground. The pool is open to hotel guests only. Alternative: visit the restaurants for lunch (around $50) then explore the SkyPark (excluding the pool area). The SkyPark includes an infinity pool, 3 heated Jacuzzis, a mini chocolate bar and two high‑end restaurants.
  - Price: $20/14 adults/children

- ArtScience Museum
  - Address: 10 Bayfront Ave
  - Hours: Daily 10AM–10PM
  - Notes: Part of the Marina Bay Sands integrated resort; described as the world's first ArtScience museum and the largest private museum in Singapore. Hosts mainly international travelling exhibitions. The permanent exhibition "Future World" is a high‑tech interactive installation.
  - Price (single exhibition ticket for non‑residents): $17 adult, $12 senior/student/child
  - (updated Dec 2017)

- Light and Water Show (Spectra) — Marina Bay Sands
  - Times: Daily 8PM and 9PM; Fri–Sat extra show at 10PM
  - Notes: A 15‑minute nightly light and water show; can also be seen from across the bay.

- Red Dot Design Museum
  - Address: 11 Marina Boulevard, #01-01
  - Phone: +65 6514 0111
  - Email: museum@red-dot.sg
  - Hours: Mon–Thu 10AM–8PM, Fri–Sun 10AM–11PM
  - Notes: In October 2017 the museum moved to this Marina Bay location. Presents the world’s largest exhibition of contemporary design (Red Dot Design Award winners). Features a design shop and a design cafe bar with craft beer, gourmet coffee and tea.
  - Price: $6

### Gardens and parks
- Gardens by the Bay
  - Address: 18 Marina Gardens Dr (Gardens by the Bay MRT)
  - Phone: +65 6420 6848
  - Notes: Free outdoor gardens themed around plants important to Singapore history and culture. Super trees tower over the gardens and incorporate technology such as solar cells. The gardens feature event venues like the Meadow. Super tree light shows daily at 7:45PM and 8:45PM.
  - Price: Free
  - (updated Mar 2024)

- Conservatory domes (Gardens by the Bay)
  - Hours: 9AM–9PM
  - Notes: The gardens house two cooled conservatories in giant glass domes, the Flower Dome (simulating a dry‑climate area) and the Cloud Forest. (text truncated in source)

## Practical notes
- Many northern attractions around the bay (Suntec City, Marina Square, Millenia Walk, the Esplanade) are conveniently connected by covered walkways and underground malls, making transfers easy in hot weather.
- There is a shuttle bus ($3) between Bayfront and the Flower Dome (Gardens by the Bay).

- Marina Barrage (mentioned as blocking the bay from the sea).
