## 1. RAG (Knowledge Base)

**User:** What are the must-visit attractions in Singapore?

**Assistant:** Here are some must-visit attractions in Singapore:
- **Gardens by the Bay:** Famous for its Supertree Grove and the Cloud Forest and Flower Dome conservatories.
- **Marina Bay Sands:** The iconic integrated resort with a stunning SkyPark and infinity pool.
- **Sentosa Island:** A popular island resort featuring Universal Studios Singapore, beaches, and various entertainment options.
- **Singapore Botanic Gardens:** A UNESCO World Heritage site showcasing tropical flora.
- **Singapore Zoo & Night Safari:** Renowned wildlife parks offering immersive animal experiences.
